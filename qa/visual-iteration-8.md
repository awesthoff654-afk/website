## Most useful next changes

1. **Physical artwork substrate, not more geometric compositions.** Work-02 shows a flat beige board with a black polyline; work-05 a near-flat circle; work-09 flat dots. Keep minimal abstraction, but make paint/linen credible with fine woven normal response, physically scaled roughness variation, sparse irregular pigment marks and believable canvas side wrap. Ground colors currently almost uniform. Actual generated or captured high-resolution physical substrate texture is more useful than adding more regular vector marks. Avoid returning to macro triangular facets or smeared horizontal blur.
2. **Painting mounting contact.** Add a narrow physical mounting gap and soft short local shadow behind artwork, slight edge bevel/wrapped material. Preserve user instruction against dark architectural wall-floor/ceiling-wall junction shading; that instruction does not require all objects to appear ungrounded.
3. **Light transport with clean junctions.** Introduce broad, smooth wall irradiance variation from the rooflights and subtle directional light response on canvas, preserving white albedo. Current images have almost no cue of real plaster under large-area light. Do not recover contrast by dark perimeter bands or spotlight hotspots.
4. **Ceiling detail and floor response at a wide angle.** Need rooflight diffuser recess, opaque-white structural strips, fine tracks, restrained trim, and visible coherent rooflight reflections modulated by concrete roughness. Vestibule image is strongest current evidence; also capture a main-gallery wide view deliberately showing floor and ceiling after changes.
5. **Independent motion evidence before acceptance.** Restore reviewer browser or supply a normal-speed rendered recording with enough architecture, artwork and return to inspect. Static image review plus runtime completion cannot support the required cinematic quality gate.
